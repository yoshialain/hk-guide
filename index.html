Before you go

Staying at The Park Lane in Causeway Bay — solid location, right next to Victoria Park and easy access to everywhere. July is hot and humid, dress light and keep a small umbrella handy. 7-Elevens are literally everywhere so staying hydrated shouldn't be hard.

Optional day trip

Macau Day Trip

Swap in for any full day — best paired with Day 7 or as a standalone if you extend the trip.

  • Ferry from Sheung Wan Ferry Terminal to Macau (~1 hour). Book tickets in advance
  • Ruins of St. Paul's 大三巴牌坊 → Senado Square 議事亭前地 → wander the old Portuguese streets
  • Lunch: Tai Lei Loi Kei 大利來記 — famous pork chop bun, a Macau institution
  • Afternoon: Margaret's Café e Nata for the legendary Portuguese egg tart
  • Browse: St. Dominic's Church, Lou Kau Mansion
  • Optional: Walk to Taipa Village — quieter, more local Macanese feel
  • Evening: Ferry back to HK — taxis or bus to the terminal, easy
Tip Macau is a different country — bring your passport. The ferry terminal in Sheung Wan is easy to find and well-signed. Multiple departures per hour.

Swap with Day 6 or attach to any KLN day

Check HKJC website — Wednesday nights at Happy Valley

Practical tips
🚇
MTR
Fast, cheap, air-conditioned. Use for anything over 2 stops. Get an Octopus card on arrival.
🚃
Tram 叮叮
HK Island north shore only, HK$2.60 flat. Great for CWB ↔ Central hops. Pretty confusing though — follow Maps.
⛴
Star Ferry
Central ↔ TST, HK$3.40. Iconic — do not skip. Best harbour views in the city.
🚕
Taxi / Uber
Red cabs for urban, green for NT. Cheap by NYC standards. Cash is always easier. Uber works great too.
💧
Stay hydrated
July is very hot and humid. 7-Elevens are literally everywhere — use them constantly.
🌧
Small umbrella
Afternoon showers in July. Keep one in your bag — don't get caught out.
📱
Google Maps
Works great for MTR, bus, and minibus navigation in HK.
🗣
Use your Cantonese
Especially at old-school spots — locals will really appreciate it.
🍽
Queue = good
If there's a line, join it. Always worth it.
📅
Book ahead
Lin Heung Lau, Neighborhood, and Ozone all need reservations — do it before the trip.
🌙
Jet lag hack
Sun Hing Restaurant 新興食家 in Sheung Wan opens at 3am — legendary early-morning dim sum if you're up at dawn.
🧧
Octopus Card
Get one at the airport. Works on MTR, trams, buses, Star Ferry, and even some convenience stores.
